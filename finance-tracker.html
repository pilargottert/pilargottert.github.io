Personal Finance Tracker

A 12-tab Excel workbook built to answer one question honestly: how much do I actually have, once everything sitting in pesos and dollars is converted into a single number?

Excel SUMIFS / INDEX-MATCH Multi-currency Personal project
1

The problem

My money lives in two countries and two currencies — ARS accounts in Argentina, USD accounts in the US — and the exchange rate that actually matters isn't the official one, it's the "blue dollar" parallel rate, which can move meaningfully week to week. Budgeting apps built for a single-currency household don't handle that well: they either ignore the gap between official and blue-dollar rates, or they make you convert everything by hand before you can see a real total.

On top of that, every time I actually move money between currencies, there's a real risk of typing the wrong amount or grabbing a stale rate — and a small transcription error is easy to miss until it's thrown off a month of numbers.

2

The approach

I built the tracker as a single workbook where the Transactions tab is the only place raw data goes in, and everything else — budgets, the dashboard, goal progress — recalculates itself from there using SUMIFS and INDEX/MATCH. No pivot tables to refresh, no manual totals to update.

Live dashboard

Net worth converted to a single USD figure, this month's budget vs. actual, top 3 spending categories ranked automatically, and savings-goal progress — all formulas, no typed-in numbers.

Blue-dollar exchange log

A running log of the blue-dollar rate; the whole workbook always references whichever entry is most recent, so every conversion downstream updates automatically as the rate moves.

Source: bluelytics.com.ar / dolarhoy.com

Self-flagging conversions

Every currency exchange gets its implied rate calculated from the actual amounts moved, compared against the current reference rate, and flagged "⚠ REVIEW" if it's off by more than 3% — catching data-entry mistakes before they distort anything else.

Category budgeting

Budgeted vs. actual spend per category, pulled live from the transaction ledger, with variance calculated automatically.

Bills & cash flow

Recurring subscriptions and bills tracked in their native currency and converted to USD, with a 14-day due-soon flag, feeding into a rolling 3-month cash flow forecast.

Built to grow

Debts, Investments, and Taxes exist as structured placeholder tabs — ready to activate with real formulas once those parts of my finances are actually relevant, instead of cluttering the workbook now.
