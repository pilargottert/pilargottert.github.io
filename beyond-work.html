Puzzles

I compete in jigsaw puzzle tournaments with my mom, pairs section. Each tournament gives everyone the same puzzle to build against the clock — the design is a surprise, and it's yours to keep afterward. A podium finish also comes with a diploma (with your time on it), a medal, and a prize puzzle to take home: 500 pieces for 2nd or 3rd, 1000 for 1st.

Board Games

Always down for a game night, especially anything with a good strategy layer.

  • CatanThe classic — trading, building, and the occasional broken alliance.
  • ChessSlow, quiet, and endlessly replayable.
  • ScrabbleCompetitive in the best way.
  • BurakoRummy-style tile game, a family staple.
  • Rummy / RummikubQuick, tactical, always a good filler game.
  • Generala (Yahtzee)Fast dice game, pure competitive chaos.
  • TrucoArgentine card game built on bluffing — reading the table matters more than the cards.
  • ChinchónSpanish rummy-style card game, a go-to for a quick round.
  • Escoba del quinceTraditional Spanish/Argentine card game — quick math, quicker rounds.
