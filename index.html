About

Who I am

I'm a mechanical and piping design engineer based in Buenos Aires, currently looking for my next role in renewable energy. Over three progressive positions at an industrial surface-treatment engineering firm, I've written technical proposals, authored close to 20 technical manuals, and produced manufacturing-ready drawings and BOMs for complex industrial installations — often juggling several project sections at once and coordinating across sales, procurement, and manufacturing.
